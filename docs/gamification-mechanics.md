# 🎲 Gamification & Behavioral Economics Mechanics

## 💡 The Core Problem: Why Traditional Budgeting Fails Couples
1. **Emotional Friction**: Money is the #1 source of relationship conflict. Talking about numbers often generates defensive reactions.
2. **Income Disparity Anxiety**: When partners earn different salaries, rigid 50/50 splits create resentment or strain for the lower earner.
3. **Spreadsheet Abandonment**: Traditional financial spreadsheets feel punitive and tedious, leading to abandonment within 45–60 days.

---

## ⚙️ The Bingo2Gether Mechanism

### 1. Cumulative Sum (Gauss Arithmetic Progression)
Instead of arbitrary round numbers, financial goals are mapped into a finite numbered bingo grid:
$$\text{Total Savings Goal} = \sum_{i=1}^{N} i = \frac{N \times (N + 1)}{2}$$

*Example*:
- A **200-number board** ($\sum 1 \dots 200$) yields exactly **R\$ 20.100**.
- A **100-number board** ($\sum 1 \dots 100$) yields **R\$ 5.050**.

### 2. The Equity Algorithm (Power Law Distribution)
When two partners register their monthly incomes:
1. The system calculates a proportional ratio:
   $$R_A = \frac{\text{Income}_A}{\text{Income}_A + \text{Income}_B}, \quad R_B = 1 - R_A$$
2. Numbers on the board are distributed according to this proportional curve:
   - The higher earner draws from brackets scaled to their capacity.
   - The lower earner contributes smaller denominations with equal emotional weight and game satisfaction.
3. **Result**: Both partners experience equal psychological accomplishment without unfair financial pressure.

### 3. Habit Loop & Couple Rituals
- **Weekly Draw Ritual**: Couples designate a specific day (e.g., Sunday evening) to draw their numbers together.
- **Micro-Rewards & Streaks**: Consecutive weeks of savings build a shared couple streak, unlocking celebration badges.
- **Hard Month Mode ("Modo Meses Difíceis")**: Allows saving symbolic minimum amounts during emergency months without resetting the couple's hard-earned streak.
